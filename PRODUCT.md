# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineers, knowledge workers, students, career switchers, and high-agency individuals pursuing ambitious multi-week goals (e.g. interview preparation, certifications, deep-work projects) who struggle with cognitive overload, inaccurate time estimates, and schedule drift.

## Product Purpose

Adaptive Planner is an AI-assisted planning and goal execution platform that makes planning feel achievable, understandable, and recoverable. Rather than acting as another passive task list or punitive tracker, it answers five essential questions:
1. What am I trying to achieve? (Clear roadmaps with measurable criteria)
2. What should I do today? (Actionable daily focus blocks)
3. Am I making real progress? (Transparent milestone tracking)
4. Why is my plan not working? (Evidence-based diagnostics separating facts from hypotheses)
5. How can I recover without feeling like I have failed? (Calm trade-off simulator with actionable recovery paths)

## Positioning

The user remains in complete control: AI recommends, the scheduling engine validates constraints, and the user approves consequential changes. It replaces guilt-inducing streaks and aggressive warnings with honest trade-offs and psychologically safe recovery.

## Operating Context

- **Daily Execution Workspace:** Fast start/pause/complete actions, duration logging, and blocker capture.
- **Weekly Capacity Planning:** Conflict resolution, 15% focus compression buffers, and transition pauses.
- **Milestone Roadmaps:** Phased decomposition with clear definitions of done.
- **Recovery Simulator:** Decision-based schedule recalibration without resetting long-term goals.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Next.js App Router, React 19, JavaScript (.jsx), Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, Axios, Zustand, date-fns, Lucide React.
- **Zero Hallucination Guarantee:** All generative roadmaps and schedule reallocations are grounded in real user constraints and explicit API contracts.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA compliant typography, focus rings, semantic markup, and generous interactive targets.
- **Theme:** Warm, calm, minimal aesthetics with deep forest green accents (`#2D5A43`), cream surfaces (`#FAF9F5`), and neutral borders (`#E7E5E0`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Calm, supportive, professional, and evidence-based. No guilt-based copy (avoid "You failed" or "Overdue!").
- **Visual Personality:** Direction A (Calm and focused base) + Direction B (Personal daily execution) + Direction C (Intelligent recovery).

## Product Principles

1. **User in Control:** AI proposes and highlights assumptions; the user approves every consequential schedule alteration.
2. **Facts Before Hypotheses:** Never diagnose a missed task as laziness or poor discipline; observe timestamp evidence and present possible explanations as testable hypotheses.
3. **Calm Recovery:** When a deadline slips, offer sustainable choices (adjust scope, extend deadline, reallocate buffer) with transparent trade-offs.
4. **Bite-Sized Focus:** Keep individual tasks between 20 and 60 minutes with built-in cognitive transition buffers.
